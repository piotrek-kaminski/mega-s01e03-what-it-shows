The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so 7 April goes out as 4 July. Every channel, every tenant. This is what #4471 says it is, and the queued fix is for this.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, people type what it says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way. Only tenants on booking-v3, and only reports that came in through the form. Fixing the parser does not touch this: the form needs a hint in the tenant’s own format. The 4471 page already shows this row as “not this ticket, no ticket of its own”.

shift: a booking lands one day early or late. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” No flipped date does that; a time zone does. These are the reports the desk already listed as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The 4471 page counts them under parser and footnotes them; here they get their own row and their own ticket.

How each report is placed

  1. Count each report once: the export repeats some rows (uniqueById).
  2. Reports the desk read as a one-day move are shift.
  3. Every other report goes where its tenant puts it (causes.ts): through the form on booking-v3 is hint, anything else is parser.

The rule lives in #lib/kb/problems.ts. The table above is rebuilt from the export on every build, so the counts follow the desk as it keeps merging.

What intake got wrong

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause is the tenant’s: their locale and their form. The “intake tagged” column shows how often those disagree: hint reports tagged parser and the other way round. Don’t triage on the tag.

Why it took until now

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subjects match. “Wrong day on the invite” is the subject of all three problems, so all three went into one ticket and the count looked like one loud bug. Merging on subject is fast. It is not the same as merging on cause: triage has an explorer that shows the difference.
